Choose the burden that fits the entry

The owner chose a broad personal journal. The process follows the claims an entry makes.

Personal reflection

Begin with the author's real notes. Separate experience, feelings and interpretation. Research only external factual claims; a meaningful entry can leave a question open. Review privacy, first-person authenticity and the final wording before publication.

The two loops

Inner loop: choose a consequential evidence gap → state what would support or challenge it → search and inspect → appraise → map the claim → record what changed.

Outer loop: synthesize the support and conflicts → revise the view → deepen, broaden, pivot or conclude. Stop when material claims are supported, contrary evidence has been sought, and targeted follow-up adds no consequential change.

This adapts the requested autoresearch process for editorial work. It is not an ML experiment or a claim of systematic-review completeness. Consultant frameworks help organize ideas; they do not verify truth.

What was learned and changed

A single academic template would overburden reflection. The final workflow therefore has genres and optional story structures. A polished sample post would risk invented biography, so the worked essay uses sourced methodological material and remains a draft. A file-based review can silently become stale, so the gate hashes the article and its evidence packet after review.

The source review also narrowed claims: SANRA's medical setting does not validate this plugin; PRISMA-S helps report searches, not certify completeness; Gibbs' original book was inaccessible; and Cloudflare's general proxy recommendation does not prescribe our initial DNS-only setup.

What the gate demonstrates

Pending approval, unresolved claims, uninspected sources, missing locators, missing prose citations, duplicate IDs, changed content or evidence, stale reviews, unknown asset rights and unsafe paths are rejected in synthetic fixtures. A complete reflection can pass with an empty external-claim ledger. The real worked essay has three sources and four mapped claims, with author approval still false.

Production checks passed across nine HTML pages. The served artifact excludes drafts, the old local editor, private folders, research and plugin source. The public GitHub repository still exposes tracked drafts and notes; sensitive material belongs in ignored private/.
