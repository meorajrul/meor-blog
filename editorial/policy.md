# Editorial policy

Voice: personal, curious, clear and specific. Subjects remain broad. No invented first-person scenes, expertise, emotions, conversations, endorsements or achievements.

Evidence: distinguish external facts, personal experience, opinion, inference and calculation. Use original sources when appropriate and inspect cited passages. Record counterevidence and uncertainty. Avoid unsupported medical, legal or financial advice; such topics need current authoritative evidence and an appropriate qualified review where necessary.

AI assistance: it may organize notes, research, draft and check. The author owns the interpretation and approves publication. Disclose material AI assistance where it helps readers understand the work. Never present generated images or synthetic test fixtures as documentary evidence.

Privacy: the repository is public. Do not commit private raw journals, confidential client information, credentials or personal data about others. Clear reuse rights and permissions for material you choose to publish. Research packets may be public only after sensitive extracts are removed.

Corrections: preserve honest publication/update dates. Explain corrections that change an argument, figure or recommendation. Keep an accessible source link beside a factual claim.
