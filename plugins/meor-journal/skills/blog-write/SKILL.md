# Blog Write


Read [the packet contract](../../references/editorial-contract.md) and the author's notes. Follow explicit tone, language, length and structure requests. The project defaults to a broad personal journal, with clear prose and room for uncertainty.

Use a fitting structure from [the framework guide](../../references/frameworks.md) when useful. Draft an entry that has a reason to exist: a noticed moment, a question, a useful observation or a considered view. Avoid corporate calls to action and forced lessons in personal reflection. Vary pace and sentence length naturally.

First-person details must come from supplied notes. If they are missing, produce an outline or clearly mark author input in the draft; do not manufacture experience, feelings, expertise, quotes, travel or relationships. Keep reconstructed or illustrative scenarios clearly labeled.

For material factual claims, use inspected evidence in the ledger and place readable links next to supported statements. Match dates, populations, denominators and versions. Keep opinions and inferences visibly separate. Include limitations that change the interpretation; do not bury them in an appendix. Check that the headline and opening do not overstate the body.

Use a visual only if it answers a reader question. Preserve source data and transformations for charts, state reuse rights, and provide accessible text. Label conceptual or generated imagery. Do not manufacture data to fill a layout.

Save to the page bundle with `draft = true`. Update the claim ledger when wording changes. Produce a concise note on unresolved evidence and needed author input. Do not set author_approved or remove review issues merely because the prose is polished.

Apply [the SEO workflow](../../references/seo-workflow.md): make the topic clear in the title, opening and headings; supply a specific description and readable stable slug; link naturally to relevant published entries. Avoid forced keywords and word-count targets. Keep the article a draft and finalize SEO wording before author approval and the review digest.
