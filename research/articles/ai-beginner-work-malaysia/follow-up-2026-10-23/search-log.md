## Decisions and follow-up

The US payroll evidence supported concern about entrants but did not establish causation. Denmark and national US graduate results weakened any universal collapse thesis. Singapore supplied a nearby counterexample and measures of work redesign. The late international-paper discovery strengthened the senior/junior composition angle while requiring careful treatment of inferred adoption and instrumental-variable assumptions.

Two final targeted appraisal passes checked (1) junior definitions and statistical uncertainty, and (2) adoption measurement, sample coverage and national applicability in the international paper. These narrowed permissible wording without changing the proposed first-job focus. This is a practical editorial stopping point, not evidence of exhaustive coverage.
