## Current understanding
KRI supplies a relevant local coordination/capacity diagnosis. The contribution here is a conditional functional-review and savings framework, not a claim that KRI recommended agency closures. Malaysia has reported adoption and a 2026 digitalisation savings target. Regional cases show shared tools, pilot training, application integration and formal restructuring; these must not be treated as equivalent outcomes.

## Patterns
Tool adoption can coexist with siloed services. Shared platforms may reduce duplicated engineering while agencies keep distinct lawful functions. Structural change and technological change have separate causal paths. Time released is not automatically cash saved.
