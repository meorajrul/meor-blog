# Synthesis and stopping decision

19 October 2026. The user requested deeper MSME analysis after the KRI-first government briefing.

1. Mandate/budget loop: the 2027 federal estimates provide updated 2026 comparators and a proposed next-year baseline. Lending capital, grants, federal transfers and entity costs cannot be treated as one comparable overhead budget. Current full costs remain unverified for several institutions.
2. Audit loop: specific Micro Connector, IBBC employment and SCOPE outputs missed targets. Overall assessments and management responses qualify those findings. Audit committee status reports INSKEN/BIZME issues completed by January 2025. The 2025 halal audit supplies a concrete cross-agency administration case, but complementary grants are not automatically duplication.
3. Counterevidence loop: SMEIPA 2025 reports all selected core KUSKOP delivery agencies at/above its 70-point threshold for 2024. Official SME Corp page's embedded edition was confirmed in browser. Performance and institutional distinctiveness must therefore be separate axes. Broader programme portfolios at BSN/CGC/RISDA are weaker in this same framework; do not infer whole-institution failure.
4. Fiscal loop: public cost anchors support a conditional scenario, not a calibrated forecast. Central net 1.279m per year at maturity, −3.442m over three years. A costly reorganisation would not satisfy a three-year savings case. No loan principal, guarantees, old committed balances or existing BPMB consolidation benefit is booked as fresh savings.

Conclude this bounded pass with a report, reproducible arithmetic and an interactive quadrant. Further web searching cannot replace current agency ledgers, contracts, staffing duties, linked beneficiary outcomes, interviews and legal transfer analysis. No independent causal test, whole-agency failure finding, confirmed closure recommendation, publication or author approval is claimed.
