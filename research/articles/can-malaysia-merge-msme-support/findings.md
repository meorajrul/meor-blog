# Draft synthesis

The selected comparative outline has been developed into an essay with a conceptual hero and three data figures. Twenty inspected evidence records support twenty bounded claims. No new searches or causal results are claimed by this writing pass.

The main implication remains functional integration before legal closure: Singapore’s agency and programme merger, Thailand’s common service route, and Indonesia’s holding/shared outlets are different mechanisms. Their selected records do not establish reconciled net fiscal merger savings.

The Malaysian programme scores strengthen the distinction between programme performance and the case for a standalone institution. All three administration-transfer review candidates in the chosen sample meet the 2024 programme threshold; no invented failure is needed to discuss a transfer. The weaker measured portfolios sit in bodies with wider functions.

The central planning case produces RM1.279m mature annual net savings and a negative RM3.442m three-year balance. These arithmetic results depend on unverified cost-retirement and implementation assumptions. The illustrative scenarios have not been calibrated to observed overseas government savings.

Author review, institutional responses, a verified business journey and costed/legal transfer plans could change the essay’s recommendations. The current deliverable is a reviewable draft, not an approved policy finding or publication.
