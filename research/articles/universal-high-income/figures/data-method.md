# Chart data and limits

Three original plots redraw published numerical facts; no invented observations, distributions, uncertainty intervals, causal model or forecast. CSVs retain reference periods, units and source IDs.

Usage: 99% and 96% are government-reported rounded shares of the respective 2025 programme recipients who used their credits. They do not measure population coverage, eligibility accuracy, exclusion, poverty reduction or causal effect. Denominators differ; do not pool the two programmes.

Spending: RM5bn allocation and RM4.8bn transaction value concern monthly SARA in 2025. No multiplier, added output or causal welfare improvement is inferred. No exact residual reconciliation is attempted from rounded amounts.

Allocations: RM15bn 2026 versus RM16bn announced in Budget 2027 on 9 October 2026. Programme design changes. Both are allocations, not expenditure outcomes, not an identical-per-recipient comparison, and not projected AI revenue. The hatched 2027 bar is a published budget announcement, not our forecast.

All axes begin at zero. No significance test applies to these administrative reports. PNG, SVG and PDF are exported from the retained script and CSVs. Readable source links and caveats accompany figures in the article.
