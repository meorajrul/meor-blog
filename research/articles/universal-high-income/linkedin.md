Can Malaysia create—and share—AI abundance?

The evidence shows progress: higher output per hour, a recovery in real median wages, and growing electricity use by operating data centres.

But MPC’s selected study of 57 manufacturers did not establish a statistically reliable productivity advantage from greater AI spending. Higher wages, household income and cheaper essentials are also different questions.

Malaysia has capabilities to build on. The size of a dependable AI surplus—and how widely its benefits would reach households—remains unproven.
