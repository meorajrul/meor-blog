Can Malaysia create—and share—AI abundance?

Three questions matter: can we produce more, can households afford more, and can we fund a dependable share for everyone?

Malaysia’s data shows productivity gains, recovering real median wages and widely used assistance programmes. But MPC’s selected study of 57 manufacturers did not establish a statistically reliable productivity advantage from greater AI spending.

There is capacity to build on. The size of a lasting AI dividend—and how widely it would reach households—remains unproven.
