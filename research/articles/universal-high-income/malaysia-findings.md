# Malaysia extension — 25 October 2026

Question: can existing Malaysian welfare delivery support sharing future abundance?

The records demonstrate operating bank/cash channels (STR) and restricted MyKad essentials credit (SARA), with broad adult eligibility in a one-off payment. Government-reported 2025 use and purchase data establish use among programme recipients. They do not measure poverty reduction, exclusion or a permanent universal income. This is a grounded delivery foundation to investigate, not a tested abundance system.

Counterevidence: MOF acknowledged degraded terminal performance at some locations on 31 August 2025. Reported high use does not establish uninterrupted access. OECD’s 2026 Malaysia assessment calls for additional revenue and spending efficiency to improve social protection. No affordability model is adopted.

Charts retain separate 2025 recipient denominators, allocation versus transaction value, and a 2026/2027 allocation comparison with the future year expressly identified as announced. No chart is a causal-impact estimate or AI-revenue forecast. Programme definitions differ across years. No population headcount is computed by adding households to individuals or adding monthly recipients to one-off recipients.
