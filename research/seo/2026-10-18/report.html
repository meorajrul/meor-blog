What to leave alone for now

Keep the broad personal journal. Do not introduce a publishing quota, artificial minimum length, keyword-density score, mass-produced AI posts or a large taxonomy of empty pages. Google ignores meta keywords and does not prescribe a magic word count. Those shortcuts do not address the current absence of public entries. Starter guide
