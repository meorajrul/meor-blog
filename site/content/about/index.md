This is a personal journal by Meor Ajrul: a place for reflections, things learned, projects, and questions worth exploring.

Some entries are personal observations. Others examine a question through books, research, or practical examples. When an entry makes factual claims, sources belong beside those claims. Opinions and interpretations should be recognizable as such.

The aim is to write clearly, stay curious, and leave room to change a view when the evidence changes.
