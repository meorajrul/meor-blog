I'm Meor Ajrul, based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. My work brings together software engineering, solution architecture, and helping organisations put AI to practical use.

The question behind that work is straightforward: how can technology reduce manual effort and help people do more, while working with the systems they already have and keeping them in control?

## My background

I'm currently a **Pre-Sales AI Solution Architect at Singularity Connect**. My background spans hands-on software development, architecture, technical leadership, and working with businesses to understand what they need from technology.

Before this, I was **Head of Solutions** and a **Senior Solutions Architect at Singularity Aerotech Asia**. Earlier roles included solution architecture and application development at Strand Aerospace Malaysia, blockchain development at Serba Dinamik IT Solutions, and software engineering at Nazrol Tech.

That work has taken me across areas including aerospace and aviation, agriculture, financial technology, and government. Along the way, I've worked on software products, cloud and on-premises systems, and building technical teams.

I studied computer science at **Universiti Teknologi MARA**, completing a diploma and an honours degree in Netcentric Computing.

You can find my professional background and latest roles on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/meorajrul/).

## How I approach things

I enjoy helping people sharpen an idea into something they can act on, whether that means technical guidance, supporting younger talent, or learning across disciplines myself.

A few principles from my working life stay with me:

- Small, consistent improvements matter.
- Execution gives an idea its value.
- Clear goals make it easier to decide what to do next.
- Knowing when to change direction matters as much as knowing how to start.

## Why this journal

This is a place for reflections, things learned, projects, and questions worth exploring, in work and beyond it.

Some entries will start with a personal observation. Others will follow a question through books, research, or practical examples. When an entry makes factual claims, I'll link the sources; when it's an interpretation or a view still taking shape, I'll make that clear.

The aim is to stay curious, write clearly, and leave room to change my mind.
